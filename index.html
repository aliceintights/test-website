Welcome to The Orchard House Hotel

Located in the quiet hill town of Mapleford Springs, The Orchard House Hotel is a small countryside hotel designed for relaxing weekends, family visits, business retreats, and longer seasonal stays.

Our property sits beside an old apple orchard, a walking trail, and a small lake. Guests can enjoy peaceful mornings, comfortable rooms, warm meals, and simple service from the moment they arrive.
