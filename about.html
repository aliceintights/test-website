About The Orchard House Hotel

The Orchard House Hotel is a fictional boutique hotel in Mapleford Springs, a peaceful town surrounded by green hills, small farms, and forest paths. The hotel combines the feeling of a traditional guesthouse with the practical comfort of a modern stay.

Our goal is to make every guest feel rested, welcome, and well cared for. We are a good choice for couples, families, solo travelers, remote workers, and small groups.

Room types

Standard Room

A simple and comfortable room for one or two guests. Includes a queen-size bed, private bathroom, desk, Wi-Fi, TV, and coffee station. Prices start from $89 per night.

Garden View Room

A bright room overlooking the garden and orchard. Includes a king-size bed, sitting area, private bathroom, Wi-Fi, TV, mini fridge, and coffee station. Prices start from $119 per night.

Family Room

A spacious room for families or small groups. Includes one queen-size bed, two single beds, a private bathroom, storage space, Wi-Fi, TV, and mini fridge. Prices start from $149 per night.

Junior Suite

A larger room with a separate seating area, king-size bed, sofa, private bathroom, desk, Wi-Fi, TV, mini fridge, and coffee station. Prices start from $179 per night.

The Orchard Suite

Our most spacious room, ideal for special occasions or longer stays. Includes a king-size bed, living area, private balcony, large bathroom, premium toiletries, Wi-Fi, TV, mini fridge, and welcome basket. Prices start from $229 per night.

Restaurants and dining

The Apple Room

Our main breakfast room serves fresh pastries, fruit, yogurt, eggs, toast, coffee, and tea. Breakfast is served daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM.

Lantern Bistro

Lantern Bistro is our casual dinner restaurant. The menu includes soups, salads, roasted chicken, vegetarian pasta, seasonal vegetables, and homemade desserts. Dinner is served from 5:30 PM to 9:30 PM.

Nearby diners

Guests who want to explore the town can visit several nearby cafés and diners. The reception team can recommend local places for lunch, coffee, or late-night snacks.

General hotel policy

  • Check-in begins at 3:00 PM.
  • Checkout is until 11:00 AM.
  • Early check-in and late checkout are subject to availability.
  • Guests must show a valid ID at check-in.
  • Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.
  • Smoking is not allowed inside the rooms or public indoor areas.
  • Lost room keys may result in a replacement fee.

Pet policy

Small dogs are welcome in selected rooms. A maximum of two pets is allowed per room. A cleaning fee of $25 per stay applies. Pets must not be left alone in the room unless they are safely crated. Guests are responsible for any damage caused by their pets.

Service animals are welcome according to local regulations.

Frequently asked questions

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is included with most direct bookings. Some special rates may not include breakfast, so please check your booking details.

Do you have free Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available in all rooms and public areas. The network name is ORCHARD-GUEST.

Can I request a specific room?

You may request a specific room or floor, but requests are subject to availability.

Do you offer airport transfer?

Airport transfer can be arranged with advance notice. Additional fees may apply.

Can I host a small event?

Yes, our meeting room can be used for small events, workshops, interviews, and private dinners. Please contact us for availability.
